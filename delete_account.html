Account & Data Deletion

Complete control over your personal data and local application cache.

⚠️ What gets erased when you delete your account?

  • Cloud Account Records: Your authentication ID, email address, and profile details will be permanently removed from our Firebase authentication servers.
  • Local Device Storage: All locally cached wallpapers, favorites, downloaded histories, and user preferences stored on your phone will be instantly wiped.
  • Irreversible Process: Once confirmed, this data cannot be recovered.

How to Delete Your Account In-App

1
Open the WallZen app on your Android device.
2
Navigate to the Profile tab.
3
Tap on "Delete Account" at the bottom of the screen.
4
Confirm the prompt. Your cloud data and local app storage will be cleared immediately.
Google Play Compliance Note: Account deletion is fully handled within the WallZen app to ensure instant wiping of both remote database records and local storage cache. If you have uninstalled the app and wish to remove server logs, you can reinstall the app or contact support.
